## How an engagement works

1. **Discovery and audit (about 3 to 5 days):** map workflows, find bottlenecks, and estimate the automation opportunity before building.
2. **Solution architecture (about 1 week):** choose the models, integrations and workflows for the client's tools.
3. **Build and integrate (about 2 to 4 weeks):** build, connect and test against real scenarios.
4. **Go live and optimise (ongoing):** monitor, retrain on the client's data, iterate toward the agreed KPIs.

Terms stated on the website: first automation typically in about two weeks, full rollout typically 4 to 10 weeks, month-to-month with no lock-in, support after go-live, and milestones tied to the client's KPIs. Exact timelines are confirmed after the audit. You can start with one automation and scale.
